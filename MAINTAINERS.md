## Organizational Independence

NTI is an independent project. No third-party organization is represented in this
file, and no single organization holds more than one seat. The project is
committed to growing the maintainer base and to meeting the LF Decentralized
Trust diversity expectation over time (see the
[Project Lifecycle](https://lf-decentralized-trust.github.io/governance/governing-documents/project-lifecycle/)
diversity criteria).

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Maintainers are expected to:

- Review pull requests and issues in a timely manner.
- Uphold the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
- Participate in technical discussions in public (GitHub issues and pull requests).
- Help grow a diverse contributor base beyond the founding projects.

## Becoming a Maintainer

Contributors who demonstrate sustained, high-quality contributions and alignment
with the project's goals may be invited to become maintainers by rough consensus
of the existing maintainers, as described in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).
